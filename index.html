Project SHARDA

Preserving India's heritage by helping children live it.

Through workshops, community storytellers, heritage walks, traditional arts, and digital learning, SHARDA turns heritage from a chapter students read into a tradition they take part in.

Join Us Partner With Us Explore India's Heritage →

What SHARDA Stands For

Strengthening Heritage through Active Reconnection, Dialogue and Action

Why SHARDA

Heritage taught from a textbook rarely becomes heritage a child can touch.

The problem

  • Textbook-centric, exam-driven delivery
  • Disconnected from the communities it describes
  • Limited to a handful of major narratives
  • Rarely hands-on or experiential
becomes →

What SHARDA builds instead

  • Workshops led by artisans & practitioners
  • Heritage walks through real streets, temples, markets
  • A living digital archive, state by state
  • Hands-on making — weaving, script, story, song
